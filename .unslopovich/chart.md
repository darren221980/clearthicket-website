# Chart

Stack: Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS. No build step, no dependencies. Hosted on GitHub Pages at clearthicket.co.uk (CNAME).
Platforms: Web, phone to desktop. Dark only (the "forest" theme in themes.css); no light mode.
Visits: on
Screenshots: headless Chrome (C:\Program Files\Google\Chrome\Application\chrome.exe). Chrome will not open a window narrower than 500 px, so for 375 and 768, load the page inside an iframe of that width.

## Top tasks
1. Find out what an app does and install it from Google Play (home -> app page -> Google Play).
2. Read an app's privacy policy or support page, often arriving straight from the Play listing (app/privacy.html, app/support.html).
3. Learn who makes the apps and how to get in touch (about.html, contact.html).

## Users and context
Android users weighing up an app, often on their phone and often coming from a Play listing. Most care about price, privacy and whether the app works; few want technical detail.

## Brand
- Color: forest green. Primary #2FBF71, accent #86D97F, background #06140F, surfaces #0E2119 / #162B22 (themes.css, [data-theme="forest"]). The ring gradient #1E9E5A -> #86D97F comes from the logo mark.
- Type: Manrope (Google Fonts) for everything. Material Symbols Rounded for icons.
- Keep: the logo (images/brand/logo-horizontal.svg), the forest palette, the app icons as the main visuals, one-off pricing with no subscriptions.
- Voice: British English. One person (Darren) runs the studio.
- Privacy wording must match what the apps do: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, data stored on the phone; photos and text sent for AI go from the phone to Google Gemini with the user's own key.
