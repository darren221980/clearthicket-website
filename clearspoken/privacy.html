Privacy Policy for Clearspoken

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Application ID: com.clearthicket.clearspoken

In short

Clearspoken has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your voice recordings, cleaned transcripts, summaries and to-do lists are stored only on your phone. Clear Thicket does not run servers that receive or store your data. When you choose to analyse something, the recording or audio file you choose to clean up is sent straight from your phone to Google Gemini using your own Gemini API key.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or usage data, and you don't need an account to use Clearspoken. The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics or tracking libraries and no crash-reporting services.

2. Device permissions

  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Used only while you are recording a voice note. Recordings are kept in the app's private storage.
  • Foreground service (android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE): Keeps a recording you started running if the screen locks or you switch app, with a visible notification while it records. A wake lock (android.permission.WAKE_LOCK) stops the phone sleeping mid-recording.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used on Android 13 and later for the recording notification and for reminders you switch on.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Used to send your chosen recording to Google Gemini for transcription and to let Google Play handle purchases.

3. AI analysis with Google Gemini and your own key

Clearspoken uses Google Gemini to analyse content you choose. To do this you add your own free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio in Settings. Your first 5 clean-ups are free. Clearspoken Pro, a one-off purchase, unlocks unlimited clean-ups with the same key.

  • Only the recording or audio file you choose to clean up is sent, and only when you start an analysis. It travels directly from your phone to Google over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • Your API key is encrypted on your phone using the Android Keystore. It is never sent to Clear Thicket and is not included in backups.
  • Clear Thicket never sees, logs or stores your key, your recordings or the results.
  • Google handles what you send under its own terms. If your key uses Google's free tier, Google's terms allow it to use submitted content to improve its services, so avoid sending anything you'd rather keep private. See the Gemini API terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Transcripts and summaries are AI-generated and can contain mistakes. Check anything important against the original recording.

4. In-app purchases and reviews

Clearspoken Pro is an optional one-off purchase handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Clear Thicket never receives your card or bank details. The app checks Google's signed purchase record on your phone to unlock Pro. The app may occasionally ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review feature, which Google runs. We don't see who left a review from inside the app.

5. Storage on your phone and backups

Everything you save is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Android's automatic cloud backup is switched off for Clearspoken, so your data isn't copied to Google's servers by the app. You can also import an audio file (MP3, M4A or WAV) that you pick; the app makes its own private copy.

If you use Back up to a file in Settings, the app creates a backup file containing your saved items and their recordings, and saves it where you choose using Android's file picker (for example your phone's storage or Google Drive). The backup file is not password-protected, so keep it somewhere private. Your Gemini key is not included. Clear Thicket has no access to your files or your Google Drive.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • You can delete any saved item, or remove your Gemini key, at any time in the app.
  • Clearing the app's data or uninstalling Clearspoken permanently deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay until you delete them.
  • Content already sent to Google for analysis is handled under Google's terms, as described in section 3.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights to access, correct and delete personal data. Because Clear Thicket doesn't receive or hold your data, these requests are usually best handled in the app itself, as described above. For data sent to Google, contact Google using the links in section 3. You can still contact us with any question.

8. Children

Clearspoken is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
