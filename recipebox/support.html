Setup & Guides

How to import recipes from Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube

  1. While viewing any recipe video or post on Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube, tap the Share button.
  2. In the Android system share sheet, select RecipeBox.
  3. RecipeBox will automatically extract the ingredients, step-by-step directions, prep times, and servings into a clean, ad-free card.
  4. Alternatively, you can copy any recipe URL and tap "Import from URL" inside RecipeBox.

How to get a free Google Gemini API key (BYOK)

  1. Open Google AI Studio in your browser and sign in with your Google Account.
  2. Click the blue "Get API key" button in the sidebar.
  3. Click "Create API key" and copy your key (starts with AIzaSy...).
  4. Open RecipeBox, tap Settings > Kitchen Defaults > AI Provider & Model, paste your key, and tap Save. You now have direct, unmetered AI access!

Restoring your Pro Lifetime Unlock

If you reinstall RecipeBox or upgrade your phone, ensure you are logged into Google Play with the same Google Account used for your original purchase. In RecipeBox, open Settings and tap "Restore Purchase". Your lifetime Pro status will be restored immediately with zero additional charges.

Backing up and exporting your recipes

RecipeBox gives you 100% data sovereignty. Go to Settings > Backup & Export to create a portable ZIP archive containing your full recipe database and photos. You can save this archive directly to Google Drive, an SD card, or local storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does RecipeBox work offline without internet?

Yes! All saved recipes, Cook Mode, kitchen timers, unit conversions, and meal planning operate 100% locally from your device's internal SQLite database without an internet connection. Only AI recipe extraction requires an internet connection.

How does Hands-Free Cook Mode work?

Cook Mode keeps your screen awake and displays large, high-contrast instruction cards. When speech recognition is active, you can simply say "Next step", "Previous step", or "Start timer" to navigate while your hands are covered in flour or cooking oil.

Why is there a 5-import starter limit?

RecipeBox provides 5 free cloud AI imports upon installation so you can experience smart extraction instantly. To ensure ethical pricing with zero recurring subscriptions, a single one-time Pro purchase of £4.99 unlocks unlimited imports forever using your own API key.
