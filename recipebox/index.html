Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does RecipeBox require a login or account?

No! RecipeBox has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no cloud passwords. Your recipe data stays on your phone in an encrypted local database.

How do I get an API key for BYOK?

You can generate a free Gemini API key in less than a minute from Google AI Studio. Simply paste it into RecipeBox's settings to enjoy direct AI features with zero ongoing fees.

Can I export my recipes if I change phones?

Yes. RecipeBox includes full export and restore features. You can generate a backup archive and save it to Google Drive or local storage at any time.
