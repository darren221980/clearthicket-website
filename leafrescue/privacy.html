Privacy Policy for LeafRescue

Effective Date: October 27, 2026 · Application ID: com.clearthicket.leafrescue

Summary: 100% Client-Side BYOK Architecture

LeafRescue is built from the ground up to respect user privacy. Clear Thicket does not operate backend servers that store your plant photos, garden records, or API credentials. All data is stored locally on your device, and AI requests are sent directly from your device to Google Gemini using your personal API key.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, harvest, or transmit your name, physical address, email address, phone number, location coordinates, contacts, or photos to Clear Thicket. We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, data broker tracking libraries, or analytics SDKs in LeafRescue.

2. Device Permissions Used by LeafRescue

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used only when you choose to take a photo of a sick leaf within the app for diagnostic analysis. Photos are processed in memory and stored locally in private app storage.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used on Android 13+ devices solely to deliver local daily watering reminders when plants in your local garden are due for care.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Used exclusively to make direct HTTPS calls to Google Gemini's multimodal API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) and to connect to Google Play Billing services.

3. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) & Google Gemini AI

To perform plant diagnoses, you provide your own Google Gemini API key obtained from Google AI Studio. Your API key is stored securely in the hardware-backed Android Keystore system with AES-256-GCM encryption. When you perform a scan, the leaf image and diagnostic prompt are sent via direct HTTPS from your phone to Google. This transmission is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Clear Thicket never sees, intercepts, or retains your API key or inference content.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases (such as the one-time Pro Lifetime Unlock) are processed directly by Google Play Billing. Clear Thicket does not receive or store your credit card, debit card, or banking details. Google handles all financial transactions under Google Play terms of service.

5. Garden Backup & Local Storage

Your garden data (plant species, nicknames, watering intervals, care history) is stored in a private local SQLite database on your device (Room). When using the Garden Backup feature, the app generates a standard JSON file and uses the native Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) to let you choose where to save it (e.g., Google Drive, local storage, or Downloads). Clear Thicket cannot access your external storage or files.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data resides strictly on your local device:

  • You can clear your Gemini API key or reset scans directly in the app's Settings screen.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately and permanently erases all locally saved plants, care notes, database records, and encrypted Keystore preferences.

7. Children's Privacy

LeafRescue is a general-audience plant care tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
