Setup & Guides

How to get a free Google Gemini API key

  1. Open Google AI Studio in your browser and sign in with your Google Account.
  2. Click the blue "Get API key" button in the left sidebar or top right.
  3. Click "Create API key" and select or create a project.
  4. Copy the generated API key (it begins with AIzaSy...).
  5. Open LeafRescue on your Android phone, go to Settings, paste the key into the API key field, and tap "Save Securely".

Backing up and restoring your garden

LeafRescue includes zero-lock-in JSON export and import:

  • To Backup: Go to Settings > Backup & Restore > tap "Export Garden (JSON)". Android's file picker lets you save directly to Google Drive, Downloads, or local files.
  • To Restore: Install LeafRescue on any device, go to Settings > tap "Import Garden (JSON)", and select your backup file. All plants, diagnoses, and care notes will immediately restore.

Restoring your Pro Lifetime Unlock

If you reinstall the app or switch to a new phone, ensure you are signed into the Google Play Store with the same Google Account used for the purchase. In LeafRescue, open Settings and tap "Restore Purchases" (or tap it in the paywall dialog). Your unlimited access will be verified instantly without charging you again.

Common Troubleshooting

"Diagnosis Failed: API Key Error"

This typically means your Google Gemini API key has expired, was mistyped, or lacks access to the Gemini 3.8 Flash model. Visit Google AI Studio, generate a new key, and paste it into LeafRescue Settings. Make sure there are no accidental spaces before or after the key.

Watering notifications are not firing

On Android 13 and above, you must grant the Notification permission. In LeafRescue Settings, look at the Watering Alerts card. If it says "Permission Needed", tap to grant it. You can also tap "Send Test Watering Alert" to immediately verify that notifications appear on your status bar. Also check that Android's aggressive "Battery Optimization" is set to "Unrestricted" for LeafRescue.

How can I send diagnostic logs with my inquiry?

In LeafRescue Settings, scroll down to the Support & Feedback card and tap "Email Developer". The app will automatically pre-populate an email to leafrescue@clearthicket.co.uk with your device model, Android OS version, and app build details to help us solve your problem faster.
