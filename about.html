👨‍💻 Behind the Studio

Hi, I'm Darren Bromwich.

I am an independent software engineer and creator based in the United Kingdom. I founded Clear Thicket to build native Android applications that respect your attention, your device, and your hard-earned money.

Why "Clear Thicket"?

Modern app stores have become a dense, overgrown thicket of predatory £40/year subscriptions, invasive analytics trackers, forced sign-up screens, and bloated web wrappers.

Clear Thicket stands for cutting a clean path through that noise: providing clarity, simplicity, and honest digital craft that does exactly what it promises without compromise.

What Drives My Work

1. Client-Side BYOK & Zero Telemetry

Most modern apps require sending your photos, running routes, or personal notes to a private cloud server. My apps run locally on your phone. In LeafRescue, your Gemini API key is encrypted directly inside the hardware Android Keystore using AES-256-GCM. Your data never touches a Clear Thicket server because we don't have one to store it on.

2. Rejecting the Subscription Treadmill

You shouldn't have to pay a recurring monthly or yearly fee just to keep a houseplant alive or run a 5K. Every Clear Thicket app offers a generous free tier followed by a fair, transparent one-time lifetime unlock via Google Play. Once you unlock it, it is yours forever.

3. Native Android Excellence

I build strictly with native Android technologies: Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room SQLite, and WorkManager. No bloated cross-platform engines. This guarantees instant startup, 120Hz smooth scrolling, offline-first reliability, and battery efficiency.
