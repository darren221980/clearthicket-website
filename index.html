How the apps work

Your data stays yours

No accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your recipes, plants, repairs and runs are stored on your phone. When you ask an app to read a photo or a recipe, it goes straight from your phone to Google Gemini using your own key. It never passes through a Clear Thicket server, because there isn't one.

Pay once, if at all

Every app has a free allowance. If you want more, there's a single one-off Pro unlock. No subscriptions.

Made for Android

Written in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, so the apps look and behave like the rest of your phone, and work offline wherever they can.

Who makes them

I'm Darren Bromwich, an independent developer in the UK. I started Clear Thicket to build the apps I wanted on my own phone: focused tools that do one job, respect the person using them, and don't nag for a subscription.
