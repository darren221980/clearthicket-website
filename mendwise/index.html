How it Works

From Problem to Fixed in Four Steps

Step 1

Snap the Problem

Photograph the drip, crack, or broken fixture. Add a short note if you want to give extra context.

Step 2

Review the Diagnosis

Check the issue, cause, tools, steps, safety notes, and cost estimates. Edit anything before you save it.

Step 3

Work Through the Checklist

Follow the numbered steps and tick each one off. A progress bar shows how far through the job you are.

Step 4

Mark it Done

Move the project to Completed and keep it as a record of what you fixed and how.

info

Mendwise gives guidance, not a professional inspection. For gas, mains electrics, structural work, or anything you're unsure about, use a qualified tradesperson.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need a Google Gemini API key?

Mendwise sends your photo directly from your phone to Google Gemini for analysis, so there's no middleman server. Google's free tier covers everyday personal use. Create a key in about a minute at Google AI Studio, then paste it into Mendwise Settings.

Can Mendwise tell me when to call a professional?

Yes. Jobs that aren't safe or sensible to DIY are rated Professional Recommended, and every guide includes safety warnings. You can also set your DIY experience level in Settings so guides match your skills.

How do I move my projects to a new phone?

Go to Settings > Workshop backup & restore and tap Export backup. Save the file to Google Drive, Downloads, or an SD card, then use Restore on your new phone. Photos come with it.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Pro back?

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account you bought Pro with, open Settings, and tap Restore purchases. There's no extra charge.
