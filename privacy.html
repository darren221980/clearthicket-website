🔒 Studio Privacy Commitment

Clear Thicket Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026 · Website: clearthicket.co.uk

Our Fundamental Principle

At Clear Thicket, we believe your personal data belongs strictly to you. We do not track your activity, we do not sell your information, and we do not build systems designed to harvest user behavior. Both our website and our applications are engineered to function client-side with minimal external dependencies.

1. Website Data Collection (clearthicket.co.uk)

Our website does not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or third-party advertising scripts. When you visit our site, standard server access logs (such as IP address, requested URL, browser user agent, and timestamp) may be recorded temporarily by our static hosting infrastructure strictly for operational security and network health.

2. Mobile Applications & Specific Policies

Each mobile application published by Clear Thicket operates under its own dedicated privacy policy:

  • LeafRescue: Operates entirely client-side with Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) Google Gemini AI integration and local Room SQLite storage.
    View LeafRescue Privacy Policy →
  • BreatheRun: Operates offline without user accounts. GPS location data is processed locally during runs for pace and distance calculation.
    View BreatheRun Privacy Policy →

3. Direct Communication & Inquiries

When you email us (e.g., via contact@clearthicket.co.uk or leafrescue@clearthicket.co.uk), we only use your email address and message contents to answer your inquiry and provide technical assistance. We never add your email to marketing newsletters without explicit consent, and we never share your email with third parties.
